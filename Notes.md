# Build Notes & Reflections

## Overview

This diorama was built as a hands-on exploration of physical space, motion, and visual interaction rather than a fully specified engineering project.

Many decisions were made experimentally during assembly rather than through predefined calculations.

---

## Key Challenges

- Managing limited internal space while maintaining visual clarity
- Balancing movement, lighting, and structure without overcrowding the scene
- Iterating on physical layout where small changes had outsized visual impact

---

## What I Learned

- Physical constraints often surface issues that are not obvious in conceptual design
- Visual effects such as motion and light are best tuned experimentally at small scale
- Assembly order and access matter as much as the individual components

---

## What I Would Do Differently

- Prototype layout earlier using temporary materials
- Separate structural and visual elements more clearly
- Allow additional clearance for adjustment and maintenance

---

## Why This Project Matters

This build reinforced the importance of grounding design ideas in physical reality and highlighted the tradeoffs between conceptual intent and material constraints.

It complements system-level design work by emphasizing execution and iteration.
