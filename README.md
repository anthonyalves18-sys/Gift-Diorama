# Interactive Diorama — Physical Prototyping Study

This project is a physical diorama created to explore spatial design, mechanical motion, and visual storytelling at a small scale.

Unlike purely digital or conceptual projects, this work focuses on hands-on problem solving, material constraints, and iterative assembly.

---

## Project Scope

The diorama was developed as a practical experiment in:
- Translating ideas into physical form
- Managing space and scale
- Integrating mechanical or interactive elements
- Iterative prototyping and refinement

This repository documents the build as a **physical prototyping study**, not a polished product or production-ready system.

---

## What This Project Explores

- Physical layout and composition
- Mechanical interaction and movement
- Structural stability at small scale
- Visual balance and focal points
- Assembly constraints and tolerances

---

## What This Project Does Not Claim

- No finished commercial product
- No optimized electronics or firmware
- No finalized manufacturing design

The emphasis is on learning through making rather than completeness.

---

## Artifacts

This repository may include:
- Photos of the completed diorama

Documentation is intentionally lightweight to keep focus on the physical object itself.

---

## Reflection

Building the diorama highlighted the differences between conceptual design and physical execution, particularly around space limitations, material behavior, and assembly order.

The project complements system-level design work by grounding ideas in real-world constraints.
